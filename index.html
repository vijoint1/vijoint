Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you service?

VIJOINT is based in Pretoria and covers the greater Pretoria and Centurion areas as well as clients across Gauteng.

Can I book just one service, or do I need the full setup?

Either — pick exactly what you need in the booking form below, from a single DJ to full event production covering sound, stage, lighting, tents and more.

How do I book VIJOINT for my event?

Fill in the booking form below with your date, service and venue, or message us directly on WhatsApp using the button at the bottom of the page.

How far in advance should I book?

Book as early as possible to secure your preferred date, especially for weekend weddings and peak-season events.
